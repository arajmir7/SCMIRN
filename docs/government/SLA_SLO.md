# SLA and SLO status

The proposed operating model is in [`../operations/SLO.md`](../operations/SLO.md) and [`OPERATIONS_MODEL.md`](OPERATIONS_MODEL.md). No production operator, service commitment, measured baseline, or alert response is verified.

Before making an SLA, assign an accountable service owner; choose observable availability, latency, error, dependency, retention-lag and handoff indicators; define measurement window and exclusions; publish incident/status communication; and verify alerts reach a staffed responder. Do not use simulated dashboard figures as service performance.
