Canonical criterion-by-criterion evidence is maintained in [`../compliance/GIGW_3_MATRIX.md`](../compliance/GIGW_3_MATRIX.md). That matrix is an internal self-assessment, not a certification or conformity finding.

**Overall status: `NOT_TESTED` for conformity.** Do not label GIGW compliant or certified.
