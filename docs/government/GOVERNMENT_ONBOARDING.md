# Government onboarding checklist

## Sponsorship and scope

- Name the sponsoring department, service owner, data owner, legal/content reviewer, security lead, operator and citizen grievance contact.
- Approve jurisdiction, service set, public-facing claims, success measures and out-of-scope services.
- Sign data-sharing, processing, support, incident and change-control agreements as applicable.

## Evidence and integration

- Supply current official source documents/URLs, authority, effective dates, hashes, review/supersession rules and publication approvers.
- Provide API contracts, written authorization, sandbox credentials and test records for each connector; specify receipt/status proof and failure handling.
- Approve identity assurance, staff roles, MFA, tenant model and role revocation.

## Readiness gates

- Complete privacy/legal review, notice/consent, data inventory, retention and residency decisions.
- Pass security, accessibility, API, migration, authorization and abuse evaluations; generate signed artifact evidence.
- Approve production topology, monitoring and staffed alerts, backup/restore, DR, incident contacts, rollback, support and citizen correction/grievance paths.
- Start with synthetic data; do not load citizen data until the signed pilot gate is satisfied.

No onboarding step is complete by virtue of this checklist. Record approver, evidence, date and expiry in the deployment's controlled repository.
