# DPDP readiness matrix

**Assessment date:** 2026-10-03. **Status:** privacy engineering inventory only. This is not a legal opinion or a claim of DPDP compliance. The official [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 Gazette](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf), dated 13 November 2025, specifies staged commencement: rules 1, 2, 17–21 on publication, rule 4 after one year, and rules 3, 5–16, 22–23 after eighteen months. Which provisions apply to a future deployment and how the Act applies require current counsel review.

The field-level inventory is [`../government/data-governance-registry.yaml`](../government/data-governance-registry.yaml). The current schema snapshot enumerates **471 mapped columns across 35 SQLAlchemy tables** and records **294 potentially personal/linkable candidate fields**: 273 await accountable approval and 21 have explicit candidate exemptions. New mapped fields fail the parity check until inventoried and classified. Existing personal-field entries remain `INVENTORIED`, not `APPROVED`. The current privacy/data-owner work is split into 273 field approvals, 35 table-governance decisions, and one profile approval in the [production blocker ledger](../release/production-blockers.yaml). Owner, processor, region, necessity/purpose, notice, retention, deletion, access/export/correction and grievance handling require both engineering implementation and accountable review.
